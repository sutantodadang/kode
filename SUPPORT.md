# Support

## Before you ask

1. Run `kode doctor`. It checks config, auth, engines, the local router, git, and your environment, and usually names the problem.
2. Check the [docs](docs/README.md), especially the troubleshooting sections in [Install](docs/howto-install.md) and [Auth providers](docs/howto-auth-providers.md).
3. Make sure you are on the latest release: `kode update`.

## Community support

- **Bugs**: open a [bug report](https://github.com/sutantodadang/kode/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with `kode --version` and the `kode doctor` output.
- **Feature ideas**: open a [feature request](https://github.com/sutantodadang/kode/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Questions**: open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/sutantodadang/kode/issues/new/choose) and describe what you tried.
- **Security**: never in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Community support is best-effort.

## Enterprise support

Commercial licensees get priority email support. See [LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md) for how to get a commercial license.
