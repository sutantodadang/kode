# Commercial Licensing

Kode is licensed under AGPL-3.0. That license is free to use, including for internal business use, as long as you meet its obligations: source disclosure for network-accessible modified versions, and copyleft on derivative works.

If your organization cannot meet those obligations, a commercial license is available.

## What a commercial license grants

- Proprietary use of Kode without AGPL's source disclosure requirement.
- No copyleft obligation on your derivative work.
- Priority support, with SLA terms negotiable per agreement.

## What it does not change

AGPL-3.0 remains available to everyone, free of charge, under its own terms. A commercial license is an alternative for organizations that need different terms, not a replacement for the open license.
